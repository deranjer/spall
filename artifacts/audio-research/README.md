# Realistic nature audio candidates

User direction (2026-10-14): realistic nature sounds by default, with candidates
for every current biome and weather sounds collected for future use.

This is a listening and selection collection, not an audio runtime or weather
implementation. Downloaded candidate audio stays local and is ignored by Git.
Collection manifests distinguish actual downloads from source-page candidates.

- `terrestrial/`: Meadow, TemperateForest, BorealForest, PineForest, Steppe,
  Alpine, Scree, Snow, Desert.
- `water-weather/`: Ocean, Beach, Riparian, Swamp; future rain, thunder, wind,
  and snowstorm layers.

Prefer CC0 field recordings; CC-BY candidates need attribution. Keep source and
license evidence with each selection. A verified license or download does not
establish that a recording has been listened to or is suitable for a seamless
loop. Review candidates before promoting them to shipped assets.

Use separate environmental layers where possible: wind and vegetation beds,
intermittent wildlife calls, local water sources, and weather. Shared layers can
serve multiple biomes; sparse biomes do not need continuous wildlife audio.
