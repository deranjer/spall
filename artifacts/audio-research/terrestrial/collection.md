# Terrestrial biome sound research

This is a source and license research pass, not a runtime-ready or auditioned sound pack. The manifest records individual source pages, license evidence, download URLs, and local files. None of the clips has been listened to in this pass, so no audible-quality claims are made.

Five original audio files were fetched (about 9.1 MB total): meadow pipit, woodland ambience, pine-forest wind, the Commons “SnowFall” clip, and gravel footsteps. All are under this folder. The first three form the strongest natural field-recording set; the other two are provisional detail layers. Forest ambience is dedicated to the public domain by its author. The other downloaded files are CC BY or CC BY-SA; preserve attribution, and review ShareAlike implications before redistribution or adaptation.

| Biome | Coverage | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meadow | Meadow pipit call | Downloaded; metadata reviewed, not auditioned |
| TemperateForest | Woodland birds, wind, insects and crows | Downloaded; author notes recording noise |
| BorealForest | Pine canopy wind, shared with PineForest | Downloaded; the source describes 25 m/s wind, so treat as a storm layer |
| PineForest | Pine canopy wind | Same shared recording as BorealForest |
| Steppe | Dry grass in wind | Strong CC0 Freesound field-recording candidate shared with Desert; download endpoint returned HTML instead of audio, so no file was retained |
| Alpine | Sparse calls in alpine meadow | 7.6-second Common Chiffchaff call at 1,900 m; CC BY-SA 4.0; metadata only, original download blocked by Wikimedia 429 responses |
| Scree | Gravel footfalls | Downloaded, CC BY-SA 3.0; optional close foley only, not a scree ambience bed |
| Snow | Falling-snow texture | Downloaded CC BY 3.0 candidate, but uploader provides little recording context; needs listening and suitability review |
| Desert | Dry grass in wind | Same CC0 Chile desert field recording candidate as Steppe; not downloaded because the source endpoint returned HTML |

Gaps remain for quiet wind in ordinary grassland, low-intensity boreal/pine ambience, sparse snowfield air, and high-altitude scree ambience. The pine recording is very windy, and the snow recording has sparse metadata. No layers were synthesized or altered. The rejected “Desert night” result is described by its uploader as music, so it was excluded; a desert train recording was also excluded as transport noise.

See [manifest.json](manifest.json) for exact attribution and source details. Keep any eventual integration limited to the source license terms, especially CC BY-SA requirements.
