# Spall water and future weather audio research

This is a source shortlist and two original files for later evaluation. The recordings have not been auditioned; no audible-quality claims are made. `manifest.json` has one record per candidate and marks each item as listened or metadata-only.

## Collection status

- 2 originals downloaded: `lake-ontario-waves.ogg` (16.6 MB, Wikimedia Commons, public-domain dedication by uploader) and `rhumphries-thunderstorm.ogg` (2.99 MB, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0; attribution is in the manifest).
- 14 additional candidates recorded from individually checked Freesound file pages; none downloaded because those pages require login for original-file downloads. Preview media was not saved or treated as original audio.
- Total downloaded audio: 2 files, approximately 19.6 MB. Shortlist: 16 candidate records (including both downloaded originals).
- No candidate has been listened to. All details on likely content derive from creator descriptions and page metadata.

## Coverage

Ocean, beach, flowing river/stream and lakeshore waves have candidates. Swamp has two distinct candidates: one described as insect/frog ambience and another recorded at a pond/wetland setting. Weather candidates cover light and heavy rain, rain on foliage, rain on street/ground, distant thunder, a thunder crack, gentle and strong wind, and a snowstorm effect. These weather records are for future use only.

## Gaps and caveats

- Freesound blocked original downloads behind login. The files remain source-page candidates until downloaded through normal authorized access.
- The one lake recording is waves on Lake Ontario's shore, not a quiet inland still-water bed. A calm lake ambience is still a useful gap.
- The strong-wind choice needs auditioning to assess whether it suits outdoor game ambience. The snowstorm choice is only a secondary designed fallback: it is computer-generated and described as heard indoors, not a realistic field-recording default. Natural blowing snow remains a gap.
- The light-rain, rain-on-street and open-window recordings may include urban reflections or incidental traffic. Suitability needs review; a separate natural soil/puddle close-up layer would improve coverage.
- Loop suitability, noise floor, edit points, and in-game layering remain unassessed because no files were auditioned.
