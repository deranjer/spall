# ENG-268 winter ice: server cost (2026-10-25)

Headless `ice_probe` (release build). Setup for every run:
- world `natural`, seed 1, size 4096 (1 km), generated in winter;
- one player at the spawn;
- column water on its worker, with off-thread staging;
- split transfer IDs reserved as the server reserves them;
- real-time 60 Hz for 240 s.

A world that starts in winter is frozen before it is served
(`GeneratedScene::freeze_water`). In play, a freeze or melt edit goes in at most
once every 2 s.

## Startup freeze

| columns | ice cells written | time |
|---|---|---|
| 0.25 m (default) | 640,361 | 3.0 to 3.3 s |
| 1 m | 716,799 | 2.3 s |

Before this freeze, the 0.25 m world's setup took about 28 s.

## Tick cost

`FIRST_DIG=1` adds one small cut at 10 s. That gives a no-ice baseline the
same first-edit cost: the first terrain edit detaches the 69 floating worldgen
fragments.

| no ice | - | 0.99 ms | 4.67 ms | 29.46 ms | 1.01 ms |

| no ice + first dig, 1 m | - | 1.64 ms | 4.11 ms | 34.52 ms | 1.11 ms |

Ticks that carry a play-time ice commit (0.25 m columns): p50 9.2 to 9.5 ms.
`tick.ice` averages under 0.01 ms.

## Target: tick p99 within about 1 ms of the no-ice baseline

- **0.25 m columns.** Ice p99 was 6.44 ms and 5.45 ms.
  - Against the 6.6 ms baseline quoted for this ticket, both runs are within the target.
  - Against the plain no-ice run (4.67 ms), the difference is +1.8 and +0.8 ms.
  - Against no ice + first dig (5.82 ms), the difference is +0.6 and -0.4 ms.
  - The two ice runs differ by 1 ms from each other, which is about the size of run-to-run noise.
- **1 m columns.** Ice p99 was 4.03 ms against 4.11 ms for its first-dig baseline.
- **Where the rest of the cost comes from.** Iced terrain alone adds nothing (startup-only run: p99 4.33 ms). Most of the extra p50 and physics time is the fragments that the first play-time edit detaches; any dig would cause the same.
- **p99.9 and max.** The 1 m ice run's p99.9 (105 ms) and every run's max come from that one detaching commit (about 215 to 280 ms) and from the first tick.

Before these changes (ice committed edit by edit in play, one edit every 4th
tick), the 0.25 m run had p99 15.98 ms and placed 156,118 cells in 2,113
commits over 240 s.
