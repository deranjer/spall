# ENG-268 winter ice: server cost (2026-10-25)

Headless `ice_probe` (release, one player at the spawn, winter, column water on
its worker, off-thread staging, split transfer IDs reserved as the server does,
real-time 60 Hz), world `natural` seed 1 size 4096 (1 km), 240 s each.

| run | ice cells / commits | tick p50 | tick p99 | tick p99.9 | physics mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no ice (`NOICE=1`) | 0 / 0 | 0.96 ms | 6.61 ms | 15.61 ms | 0.95 ms |
| ice, 0.25 m columns (default) | 156 118 / 2 113 | 2.18 ms | 15.98 ms | 25.15 ms | 1.81 ms |
| ice, 1 m columns | 2 158 / 54 | 1.79 ms | 6.72 ms | 11.90 ms | 1.18 ms |

- Ticks carrying an ice commit (default columns): p50 7.45 ms, p99 18.82 ms.
  `commit.total` mean 4.2 ms. Planning (`tick.ice`) mean under 0.01 ms.
- At 0.25 m columns ice was still forming at 240 s (world-wide, nearest regions
  first), one edit every fourth tick; the cost lasts while ice forms and stops
  when it has. With 1 m columns ice formed within 60 s.
- Ticks without ice commits are also slower (p50 2.0 vs 1.0 ms, physics mean
  1.8 vs 0.95 ms): the first terrain edit detached 69 floating worldgen fragments
  (snow, stone, slate, sandstone; structure support is world-wide), which then
  fall as bodies, and the ice adds terrain collider work. Any first dig would
  detach the same fragments.
- Startup spikes (max 560 to 980 ms) are the first ticks of every run, ice or
  not.

Target was "no noticeable cost". Planning meets it. The commits do not fully:
the p99 tick roughly doubles while ice is forming at 0.25 m columns.
