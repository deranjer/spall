## Increment 2: component operators and candidate restart payload

The branch incorporates merged main `7278376` (PRs 184 and 185) through merge
commit `c75e5cd`. The prototype now adds one pressure row per open component,
matched portal volume fluxes, density normalized by actual open capacity,
gravity along centroid connections, an explicit pressure gauge per closed

## Increment 1: geometry and seeding

## Design constraint and implemented representation

A single capacity scalar cannot represent a thin wall inside a coarse cell:
the open spaces on opposite sides become one pressure/transport unknown. Face
aperture averages alone also invent a connection when openings on adjacent
cells do not line up. Both are forbidden shortcuts for player-built dams.

`spall_fluid::cut_cell::CutCellGeometry` consumes an immutable, fully resident
`SolidBoundary` snapshot. It preserves every six-connected open component
inside each coarse cell (factor 1 through 8), each component's integer voxel
capacity, centroid and vertical layer counts. Matched open fine faces connect
components across coarse-cell boundaries; diagonal contact and misaligned
openings do not connect. Domain sides have no portal. No new physics engine,
ECS entities, global state or runtime dependency is introduced.

Component and portal ordering is deterministic. Array indices belong only to
this snapshot and must not be persisted as stable identities. Geometry anchors
are fine global coordinates, not ECS or library IDs. Explicit fine-cell,
component and portal limits fail rather than returning reduced topology. Worst
case component count approaches the fine-grid open count; coarsening alone does
not guarantee a performance bound. Build queues visit at most one coarse cell.

`aggregate_amounts` validates fine-domain water fractions and sums into each
component's actual capacity. Water overlapping solid, invalid/out-of-domain
amount vectors and non-finite values fail. No seed volume is discarded into a
majority-solid coarse cell, filled into omitted solid space, or counted as air.
Layer counts give exact open volume below a horizontal surface; they are not
a general advection/free-surface model.

## Geometry acceptance tests

Seven tests in `crates/spall_fluid/tests/cut_cell_geometry.rs` cover:

- One-voxel walls inside coarse cells at factors 3-8 keep separate spaces even
  though the old majority mask loses the wall.
- A one-voxel opening reconnects the spaces.
- Apertures require matching fine voxels on both sides; misaligned openings
  remain disconnected, while a matching opening has exactly one fine face.
- The unaligned stair fixture from the previous investigation retains all
  138.375 m3 of seed water. Saturated open components contain water rather than
  fictitious air, including majority-solid coarse cells.
- Partial horizontal surfaces integrate correct solid-excluding capacity.
- For all 256 patterns of eight occupancy decisions extruded along Z, component
  graph reachability matches independent fine-grid flood fill. Domains cross
  negative brick coordinates. Repeated snapshots compare equal.
- Invalid factors, dimensions, amounts and capacity/build limits return errors.

These verify topology and amounts, not momentum, pressure equilibrium,
displacement after edits, sealed-air behavior or durability.

## Integration decision and remaining implementation

**Keep the snapshot experimental; do not activate it in the production solver
yet.** Geometry/capacity tests pass, but an aperture/capacity-only modification
to the existing one-row-per-coarse-cell solver would reconnect the isolated
spaces that these tests prove exist. The next increment needs a component-aware
finite-volume projection and conservative transport, with gravity/pressure
terms balanced against actual open geometry. It must still pass the partial
free-surface, dam-break, trench momentum and sealed-pocket tests.

Current canonical `WaterState` stores one amount and one trapped ledger value
per coarse cell. Those cannot reconstruct which side of an internal wall held
water: summing two component amounts loses information. Before activation,
design a new explicitly versioned canonical state with geometry-scoped stable
keys and component amounts/ledger, atomically journaled with terrain. Define
old-save conversion without inventing within-cell allocation; reject ambiguous
states or use an explicit validated conversion. Presentation may aggregate
amounts but must remain separate from canonical recovery. No format change or
implicit migration is made in this increment.

Boundary/growth changes require remapping actual overlapping fine open space,
conservative displacement through matched portals and a trapped ledger when
capacity is insufficient. Immutable workers must build/solve from a consistent
terrain revision; only the owner installs a validated result at a tick boundary.
The 39 ms default-domain build cost also argues against an unconditional
owner-thread rebuild per edit. Preserve residency suspension and existing
source/outflow accounting. These remain required work, not completed claims.
