# ENG-164 (W0): walled-water failing baseline for `grid_mac`

Status: baseline recorded 2026-10-10. This documents a failure of the current
solver; it is the gate the column solver (ENG-165) and production swap (ENG-167)
must beat. Design: `docs/water-agent-handoff.md`.

## Fixture

`crates/spall_fluid/examples/water_wall_baseline.rs`, hand-built and fully
deterministic (no worldgen seed):

- Domain 40 x 20 x 12 cells at 0.25 m, closed top (`open_top: false`).
- Solid outer walls and a 4-cell-thick floor with a 4 x 2 x 6 pocket dug into
  the floor at x 30..34, z 3..9.
- Water: x 1..=12, 8 cells deep (15.0 m3), against the left wall with no
  divider, so it is released on step 1. Level equilibrium is about 2.5 cells.
- Default `MacConfig`, fixed dt 1/60 s, inline steps.

Run: `cargo run --release -p spall_fluid --example water_wall_baseline -- 30`

## Measured result (`grid_mac`, release, 2026-10-10)

| t (s) | wet edge x | largest adjacent surface step (cells) | max speed (m/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 | 4.22 | 1.45 |
| 10 | 18 | 5.57 | 0.40 |
| 20 | 18 | 3.85 | 0.18 |
| 30 | 18 | 3.44 | 0.08 |

Volume stayed 15.0000 m3 throughout (conserved). At 30 s the mid-z surface row is
`6.0 9.2 9.6 10.4 7.7 6.8 7.3 7.8 7.9 8.2 8.0 4.8 1.3 0.4 0.2 0.1 0.1 0.1 0 ...`:
the pool still stands about 8 cells deep and drops to near zero within 6 columns,
with columns 19..38 and the dug pocket dry. Surfaces above the initial 8 are
sloshing, not extra volume.

**Verdict: FAIL.** The pool does not level out (target: no wetted connection
with a head difference above 0.1 cell, per handoff W1 test 1). After 30 s the
largest step between adjacent wetted columns is still 3.44 cells, and the water
has spread to x=18 rather than the whole 38-column floor. Velocity is decaying,
so the state is heading toward a stalled wall rather than flowing on.

## Caveats

- This reproduces the stand-still wall in a small closed basin; it does not
  identify the cause. Candidates in the handoff (stationary zero-outflow cut
  caps from `ed024a9`, the 60-quiet-step sleep) are untested here.
- The pocket does not affect the result in this run (the water never reaches
  it); it is kept so the same fixture can be extended to a pocket-fill check.
- The 30 s run is one configuration. Longer runs and the generated-world case
  were not run.
- The old coarse-domain behaviour in play is unrun here; the interactive wall
  symptom remains user-observed, not measured by this fixture.

## Use as a gate

Port the same geometry to `spall_fluid::columns` as a W1 test; it must settle
with every wetted adjacent-column step at most 0.1 cell.
