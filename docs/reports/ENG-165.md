# ENG-165 (W1): standalone column solver

Status: implemented 2026-10-10, headless CPU only. Not yet wired into
`AuthoritativeWater`, regions, sleep, persistence or rendering (ENG-166..168).
Design: `docs/water-agent-handoff.md`.

## What exists

`spall_fluid::columns` (`crates/spall_fluid/src/columns.rs`): span extraction
from a `SolidBoundary`, 4-neighbour overlap-window pipe graph, virtual-pipe step
with damping and thin-film cutoff, donor outflow limit, Jacobi apply, adaptive
CFL substeps, pressure head for full spans, terrain remap with exact volume
conservation, and diagnostics (`max_wetted_head_difference`, `peak_speed_m_s`,
`overfill_volume`, `total_volume`).

Choices worth knowing:

- **Water volume per span is the one conserved quantity** (f64). Depth is
  `min(volume, height)`; anything beyond is *overfill*, which stays in the span,
  raises its head one cell per cell^3 and is pushed out by that head. Nothing is
  ever deleted; a displaced remainder with nowhere to go sits in a world ledger.
- **Pressure head** for near-full spans is relaxed (4 Jacobi sweeps per substep)
  from free surfaces and other pressurised spans. It is blended in over the top
  cell of the span (`pressure_ramp`) because a hard full/not-full switch made a
  flooded tunnel chatter (measured: flow stuck at 3.5 cells^3/s after 240 s).
  Base pressure never reads overfill, so overfill is a restoring spring and
  cannot hide water (an earlier version stored ~26 of 245 cells^3 invisibly).
- **Terrain remap** places old water into the new spans by interval overlap;
  the remainder becomes overfill in the span nearest the old surface, or moves
  to a neighbouring column if the column became solid.
- Flow, outflow limiting and apply are computed from the previous state and
  applied together; per-pipe and per-span work uses Rayon.

column buried entirely (volume relocated 5.0, 0, 2.1 cells^3, nothing unplaced).

## Not covered / risks

- No regions, sleep/wake or cost-scaling claims; step cost was not measured
  (W2). The fixtures are tiny.
- Domain edges are closed walls and the domain top is open sky; streaming
  boundaries are W2.
- Damping 1.5/s and 4 relaxation sweeps were tuned on these fixtures only. Long
  full-span chains converge slowly by design (approximate pressure).
- `apply_boundary` diffs the whole domain; per-column dirty tracking is W2.
- Thin films below 1/64 cell stop moving and remain counted in volume.
