# ENG-167 (W3): production swap to the column water

Status: **partly done, 2026-10-10.** The server's authoritative water, its
persistence, replication, client delivery and the sandbox scenes now use the
stacked-column model end to end and are tested. Three W3 acceptance items are
not met (below), so the ticket is not done. `grid_mac` and its tests are
untouched and still compile and pass; W5 deletes them.

## What changed

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| `spall_fluid::columns` | Dirty-region tracking, per-region wet-span export, restore-by-span, `fill_cell` / `drain_cell` for springs and drains, `sleep_all`, region allocation for restore. |
| `spall_sim::column_water` | `AuthoritativeColumnWater`: owns the world over the live terrain, fixed 50 ms fluid step (at most 2 per tick, debt reported), terrain-edit and residency refresh (volume conserved), springs, drains and the gated rate, per-region published frames, canonical save and restore. |
| `Simulation` | Optional `column_water` backend (config, tick order, `TickReport.column_water`, reset swap, spring-rate admin, `restore_column_water`). |
| `spall_protocol` | `ColumnWaterState` (schema 3) + `ColumnWaterDelta`; `ColumnWaterUpdate` wire record (tag 23), replica applier. |
| `spall_net` | `WireRecord::ColumnWaterUpdate`. |
| `spall_store` | Additive `checkpoint_column_water` row, `Checkpoint.column_water`, appended `JournalPayload::ColumnWaterDelta`. |
| `spall_server` | Checkpoint capture and recovery, delta journaling in the tick batch, `CustomWorld::with_column_water`, per-session byte-budgeted publisher, spring-rate admin for column scenes. |
| `spall_client` | Applies updates to a replica and publishes per-region frames (dense adapter, no region cap). |
| `sandbox` | Worldgen and editor scenes build `ColumnWaterSetup`; `into_custom_world` ships column water, not the dense domain. |

## Evidence (release, `cargo test --release --no-fail-fast` over spall_net,
spall_protocol, spall_client, spall_server, sandbox, spall_sim, spall_fluid,
spall_store): 902 passed, 0 failed, 16 ignored (the same ignored set as
before). Clippy is clean for every crate touched.

Ported scenarios (new files, the dense originals stay until W5):

| Dense test | Column test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `authoritative_water` (owned and advanced, dam breach, sealed-pocket placement) | `spall_sim/tests/column_water.rs` | held pool asleep and free; a real cut refreshes terrain then flows with volume exact to 1e-9; levels equalise and sleep after 84 s of simulated time; a block placed into water relocates 4.98 cells^3 conserved; springs, drains and the gated rate account exactly. |
| `water_persistence` | `spall_server/tests/column_water_persistence.rs` | checkpoint restores exactly; a breach journaled tick by tick, a failed commit, and a restart recover the live water at rest with the same world hash. |
| `water_replication` | `spall_server/tests/column_water_replication.rs` | a client and a late joiner receive baselines and deltas with zero errors, converge on the same regions (101,847 bytes queued over the run), and an admin reset re-seeds. |
| `water_regions`, `water_growth` | `spall_fluid/tests/column_regions.rs` (ENG-166) | regions, sleep, wake and frozen boundaries; there is no domain growth to test. |

Also: generated showcase lakes seed with 0 rejected cells, are level (head
difference <= 0.1) and cost nothing at rest; the valley scene ships column
water and no dense domain.

One real bug found by the networked test and fixed: a span that quantizes to
depth 0 was published, the client treated its region as empty and dropped the
delta base, and later deltas failed. Spans of depth 0 are no longer published
(regression test in the publisher).

## Not met

1. **Interactive play session: unrun.** Nothing here has been seen in a window.
   The client draws column water through a dense adapter into the old water
   look (the W4 mesh replaces it), so how it looks and whether it still walls
   up in play is unconfirmed. W3 acceptance needs you to check this.
2. **Server tick cost under a large release: unmeasured.** The fluid step runs
   inline on the tick thread (no worker). The ENG-166 bench showed a
   99,856-column release at p50 17 ms (1024) and 12 ms (4096) per 50 ms step,
   which is a large share of a 16.7 ms server tick when it lands on one. There
   is no worker-thread execution mode yet, and stepping is capped at 2 per tick
   with the debt reported.
3. **No interest management.** Every wet region is replicated to every session.
   Fine for the tests; a 4096 world has ~800 wet regions.

## Other risks and limits

- A terrain edit still refreshes with a whole-world pipe rebuild
  (O(allocated spans)); the clone of the terrain volume per edit tick is also
  unmeasured at world scale.
- Journal deltas can lag flowing water by up to 20 ticks (an edit tick always
  journals). A crash during motion recovers the water as of the last delta.
- Water bounded by the end of resident terrain is a closed wall, so water in a
  scene with open edges collects at the resident boundary instead of
  draining; scenes that relied on the dense domain's closed box now rely on the
  resident envelope.
- `cargo xtask check` was not run.
- The uncommitted ENG-127 stopgap in `worldgen_scene.rs` and its test are
  untouched; the old dense path still uses them.

Next: close the three gaps above (a play check, a tick-cost measurement and
likely a worker mode, interest management), then ENG-168 (rendering) and
ENG-169 (removal).
